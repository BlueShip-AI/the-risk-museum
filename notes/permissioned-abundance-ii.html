Working Paper No. 36 · 21 August 2026 · what the evidence has done since the freeze

Permissioned Abundance II: The Bifurcation

Working Paper No. 33 froze fourteen forecasts on 30 July and does not change. The month since has clarified how the thesis resolves: not one economy where either the rent holders win or the diffusion wins, but two tiers of the same economy. Everyday AI diffuses to cheap models and small firms while frontier inference concentrates where the balance sheets are, and the firms doing the substituting are the same firms expanding their frontier spend. This note lays out the receipts, and the risk case the thesis owed its readers.

V. Dimopoulos  ·  BlueShip Research

Abstract. Working Paper No. 33 said intelligence becomes abundant and the scarce factor is permission. The month since its forecasts froze has clarified how that resolves: as a bifurcation. Frontier inference concentrates among the few balance sheets that can absorb its compute cost, while everyday AI diffuses to cheap models and small firms. Diffusion at the floor, rent at the gate, one economy, two tiers. The rent claim applies to the frontier tier, the small-business boom to the everyday tier, and neither refutes the other. The strongest evidence is the join: in spend data across seventy thousand businesses, the firms substituting toward cheap routed models are the same firms still expanding their frontier spend. This note assembles the dated public receipts, names what they bear on in the frozen slate, and adds the symmetric risk the thesis owes its readers, using the one market where the price of a permission is literally printed. Nothing here touches the frozen probabilities.

1. The claim, sharpened

A month ago the honest statement of the thesis was a fork: either the permission holders keep the rent, in which case token prices stay high and the small-business story weakens, or inference prices keep collapsing, in which case the diffusion wins and the rent thins. The evidence since says the fork was a false choice. The economy is splitting into two tiers along exactly the line the thesis drew, and each side of the fork is happening, in its own tier, at the same time.

The join rows carry the argument. Substitution toward cheap models and expansion of frontier spend are happening in the same firms at the same time, which is what a two-tier structure predicts and what a single-tier reading cannot accommodate. If the gate simply captured everything, the routers would not be booming. If diffusion simply thinned the rent, the same firms would not be raising their closed-model spend.

One official series cuts at the floor story and is reported rather than hidden. The Census Bureau’s Business Trends and Outlook Survey puts national AI use at 19.8 per cent of firms as of May 2026, but 37 per cent of firms with 250 or more employees against under 20 per cent of the smallest, so the adoption STOCK is graded by size. The floor tier is a flow story, visible in formation, in router adoption, and in the Federal Reserve staff reconciliation showing 18 per cent of firms but 78 per cent of the labor force at adopting firms. Diffusion is arriving through new entrants and large employers first, not evenly.

3. What this bears on in the frozen slate

Three of the fourteen frozen forecasts read directly on the bifurcation: whether the permission holder keeps the rent, whether scarcity pricing is structural, and whether the SaaS tail preserves its public-equity economics. The Silicon Data index and the monthly Ramp letters now serve as watch series for all three. They corroborate or challenge; they never substitute for the resolution criteria declared at the freeze.

Read the two tables together and the thesis stops being abstract. The top table prices the gate’s balance sheet; the bottom table IS the gate, opening in one county and closing in the next, attaching tariffs and kill switches as it goes. Two unanimous rejections, one moratorium that became a negotiated approval, one permit granted over objections and litigated, two tariffs that pin stranded-asset risk to the load, one statutory curtailment law, and one audit of a 474 GW queue. That is differentiated permission pricing, in public, at the county and state level, while the BIS and the IMF price the same structure from above.

5. Exhibit C: a permission has a printed price, and it is revocable

The thesis owes its readers the symmetric risk, and fixed income supplies the cleanest version, because there the price of a permission is literally printed. The United States municipal tax exemption is a charter: identical credit, and the wrapper alone changes the yield. The market price of that permission is observable every day in the relationship between tax-exempt and taxable yields, and the Build America Bonds era created same-issuer pairs that priced the wrapper directly.

Municipal practitioners are currently raising the scenario that the exemption itself enters the federal fiscal debate, with the Build America Bonds structure as a blueprint for switching wrappers, taxable interest plus a direct federal subsidy, or a cap on the benefit for the highest earners. The scenario is theirs, not a forecast of this note, and the Treasury remarks circulating alongside it are relayed through commentary rather than verified transcripts, so they are not cited here as fact. What the scenario illustrates is general, and it belongs in the thesis: permission rents exist by grace of the chartering authority, and what a legislature grants a legislature can reprice. The same clause applies to every gate in Working Paper No. 33. Interconnection and siting rules change. Export regimes change. Charters are amended. A permission portfolio is long a policy regime, and its tail risk is the regime, not the cash flow.

6. What would falsify the bifurcation

Declared now, so the reading can die in public if it is wrong.

  • Router and open-model spend starts crowding out closed-model spend within the same firms in the Ramp data. The join breaks from the floor side.
  • Frontier token prices collapse toward everyday prices without the usage mix shifting. The gate fails to hold the rent, and the Silicon Data index falls on price rather than substitution.
  • The formation impulse in AI-exposed sectors rolls over to trend while the gate tier keeps compounding. The bifurcation collapses into plain concentration, and the original single-tier rent reading was sufficient after all.
  • A rail consolidation on the Stripe and OpenRouter pattern is blocked or unwound on competition grounds. That would show the permission layer answers to a still higher permission, which strengthens Exhibit C while weakening the stability of gate rents.
  • The ground-up rows of Exhibit B normalize: rejection rates fall toward zero, special tariffs unwind, and interconnection queues clear without audits. Then the permission constraint was transitional and the thesis loses its binding site.
Method
A reading of dated public evidence against a frozen thesis. This note contains no engine statistics, changes no frozen probability, and reproduces no chart from the cited letters. The muni discussion is conducted at the level of tax policy and wrappers only.
