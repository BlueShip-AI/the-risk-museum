The Dimopoulos Chain: Retiring a Volatility Model Built on Dealer
Positioning, Under Promotion Gates That Bite Backwards

V. Dimopoulos
BlueShip Research
Working Paper No. 3 · 16 July 2026

Takeaway. The signal was real, but three versions of my model never beat simpler standard methods, so I retired it.

Abstract. This paper documents three revisions of a model that forecasts volatility from states of options dealer positioning, and the promotion gates that retired the first. The underlying signal is real: dealer gamma exposure predicts the trading range of the next day beyond implied volatility (t = 7.4 in the standing classifier, which clears a stressed re-audit at 3.68). As a forecasting architecture, however, the state chain adds no value over a competent ensemble of persistence, leverage, and implied volatility. Version 1 beat implied volatility (t = 4.34) but lost to a heterogeneous autoregressive (HAR) model and failed a newly tightened gate for margin of safety (stressed t = 2.17); a neural variant was buried (t = −4.37); a pre-registered residual corrector did not improve a log HAR+VIX+GJR backbone (t = −1.07). The central result is the retirement of the founder’s namesake model by its own gates, without exception.

Keywords: realized volatility, HAR, GJR-GARCH, options dealer gamma, promotion gates, multiple testing, pre-registration, model retirement.

1. Introduction

This paper documents three versions of one model for forecasting volatility, two changes to the promotion gates, and one retirement: the complete iteration loop of the research program. Two principles organize the account. A signal that beats implied volatility does not automatically beat a competent econometric ensemble. And a research process that cannot retire its founder’s favorite model is not a process. The model under study, the Dimopoulos Chain, maps nine states, options dealer positioning crossed with realized volatility, onto the trading range of the next day; Table 1 summarizes its three revisions and their outcomes.

2. The Version 1 signal and its architectural error

The underlying signal is real. Options dealer gamma exposure predicts the trading range of the next day beyond implied volatility (t = 7.4 in the standing classifier, retained in the vault, clearing the stressed re-audit at 3.68). Version 1’s error was architectural. It entered its nine states as OLS dummies alongside the baselines, level shifts that cannot repair where a baseline errs. It also fought the wrong opponent. In volatility space the incumbent factor structure is persistence (HAR), leverage asymmetry (GJR-GARCH, the Glosten, Jagannathan and Runkle variant of GARCH), and the market’s own forecast (implied volatility). Beating a naive benchmark while losing to HAR indicates that the new information was mostly old information under a new state label, the verdict already applied to equity anomalies that die under a Fama-French plus momentum decomposition. A claim must beat the incumbent factor model, not the naive benchmark.

3. Promotion criteria that bite backwards

Midway through the program, two promotion criteria were added, proposed by the research critic and approved by the human owner; thresholds are never changed by machine.

3.1 The deflator for multiple testing

Significance bars carry a Bonferroni adjustment set by the hypothesis family’s attempt count, tracked in the state store. A survivor from a family of thirty siblings is judged as one draw of thirty. This converts the critique of multiple comparisons from a reviewer’s eyeball into arithmetic that runs on every cycle.

3.2 The rerun for margin of safety

Before promotion, every survivor is re-tested with its edge halved against the original heteroskedasticity and autocorrelation consistent (HAC) noise and its costs doubled. The mechanics matter. Naively halving the whole return series leaves the t-statistic unchanged, since t does not change with scale; the stressed test must halve the mean against the unstressed standard error, or it tests nothing. A synthetic sanity check caught this before shipping, and the lesson was filed.

Gates that apply only to future ideas are decorative, so the vault was re-audited. The gamma exposure (GEX) classifier survived (3.68). The screen for tulip mania survived at z = 5.3 on counts taken by name and month. I have since corrected that unit, because one mania is counted many times. On the honest count, one per episode, the screen clears its own 1.25 bar at 1.28, having measured 1.18 on a different data pull, so it sits on top of its threshold rather than above it. The Dimopoulos Chain v1, the founder’s namesake model, came up at 2.17 and was retired. No exception was requested.

4. Version 3: a pre-registered residual corrector

Version 3 inverted the architecture. It absorbed the strictest baseline, a HAR plus implied plus GJR model in log space, refit monthly by expanding OLS, as the backbone, and left the states the only remaining job: correcting the backbone’s residuals. Corrections were shrunk toward zero (k = 60, James-Stein flavor) across three conditionings, the nine GEX×RV cells, the sign of the Cboe Volatility Index (VIX) term structure, and a flag for state arrival. The target moved from the range of the next day to the range over 5 days, since version 1.5 had shown that daily noise drowns the signal, with leakage guards for the overlapping targets: training truncated at the refit boundary minus the horizon, HAC lags ≥ 10, and Duan smearing applied symmetrically to both models.

The specification was committed to version control before the run. Under a family deflator this is more than hygiene. Every sibling variant raises the family’s own bar, so the gate makes a single pre-registered shot the mathematically optimal research strategy. The incentive design does the disciplining.

The corrections did not improve the backbone (mean absolute error (MAE) 34.1 vs 33.7 bps; t = −1.07 on the kill test). The decomposition state by state shows the information is not uniformly zero: one state with high gamma gained 3.5 bps while a stressed state lost 4.5. A result found by inspecting the decomposition after the gate has failed is a hypothesis for a future mechanism, not a specification to refit, and it is logged as an open question.

5. Discussion: what stands after three versions

Dealer positioning carries real information relative to implied volatility alone, and that classifier remains in the vault. It does not add forecast value over a competent ensemble of persistence, leverage, and implied volatility at daily or weekly horizons under promotion criteria that require the edge to survive at half strength. A version 4 is warranted only if it brings a mechanism the ensemble structurally cannot carry. Transition rows conditioned on events (FOMC, CPI) are the standing candidate; such a model would face a bar near 3.1, because the deflator counts prior attempts against it.

Every result above is reproducible from the program records: the baseline ladder, the neural post-mortem, the memo on tightening the gates, the pre-registration committed before results and verifiable by git timestamp, and the burial. The museum publishes the process because most research shops publish only the survivors.
