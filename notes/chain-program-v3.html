The Dimopoulos Chain: Retiring a Dealer-Positioning Volatility
Model Under Backward-Biting Promotion Gates

V. Dimopoulos
BlueShip Research
Working Paper No. 3 · 16 July 2026

In one line: The signal was real, but three versions of my model never beat simpler standard methods, so I retired it.

Abstract. This paper documents three revisions of a volatility-forecasting model built on options dealer-positioning states, and the promotion gates that retired the first. The underlying signal is real: dealer gamma exposure predicts the next-day trading range beyond implied volatility (t = 7.4 in the standing classifier, which clears a stressed re-audit at 3.68). As a forecasting architecture, however, the state chain adds no value over a competent persistence, leverage, and implied-volatility ensemble. Version 1 beat implied volatility (t = 4.34) but lost to HAR and failed a newly tightened margin-of-safety gate (stressed t = 2.17); a neural variant was buried (t = −4.37); a pre-registered residual corrector did not improve a log HAR+VIX+GJR backbone (t = −1.07). The central result is the retirement of the founder’s namesake model by its own gates, without exception.

Keywords: realized volatility, HAR, GJR-GARCH, options dealer gamma, promotion gates, multiple testing, pre-registration, model retirement.

1. Introduction

This paper documents three versions of one volatility-forecasting model, two promotion-gate changes, and one retirement: the complete iteration loop of the research program. Two principles organize the account. A signal that beats implied volatility does not automatically beat a competent econometric ensemble. And a research process that cannot retire its founder’s favorite model is not a process. The model under study, the Dimopoulos Chain, maps nine options dealer-positioning by realized-volatility states onto the next-day trading range; Table 1 summarizes its three revisions and their outcomes.

2. The Version 1 signal and its architectural error

The underlying signal is real. Options dealer gamma exposure predicts the next-day trading range beyond implied volatility (t = 7.4 in the standing classifier, retained in the vault, clearing the stressed re-audit at 3.68). Version 1’s error was architectural. It entered its nine states as OLS dummies alongside the baselines, level shifts that cannot repair where a baseline errs. It also fought the wrong opponent. In volatility space the incumbent factor structure is persistence (HAR), leverage asymmetry (GJR-GARCH), and the market’s own forecast (implied volatility). Beating a naive benchmark while losing to HAR indicates that the new information was mostly old information under a new state label, the verdict already applied to equity anomalies that die under a Fama-French plus momentum decomposition. A claim must beat the incumbent factor model, not the naive benchmark.

3. Promotion criteria that bite backwards

Mid-program, two promotion criteria were added, proposed by the research critic and approved by the human owner; thresholds are never machine-changed.

3.1 Multiple-testing deflator

Significance bars are Bonferroni-adjusted by the hypothesis family’s attempt count, tracked in the state store. A survivor from a family of thirty siblings is judged as one draw of thirty. This converts the multiple-comparisons critique from a reviewer’s eyeball into arithmetic that runs on every cycle.

3.2 Margin-of-safety rerun

Before promotion, every survivor is re-tested with its edge halved against the original HAC noise and its costs doubled. The mechanics matter. Naively halving the whole return series leaves the t-statistic unchanged, since t is scale-free; the stressed test must halve the mean against the unstressed standard error, or it tests nothing. A synthetic sanity check caught this before shipping, and the lesson was filed.

Gates that apply only to future ideas are decorative, so the vault was re-audited. The GEX classifier survived (3.68). The tulip-mania screen survived (z = 5.3). The Dimopoulos Chain v1, the founder’s namesake model, came up at 2.17 and was retired. No exception was requested.

4. Version 3: a pre-registered residual corrector

Version 3 inverted the architecture. It absorbed the strictest baseline, a log-space HAR plus implied plus GJR model refit monthly by expanding OLS, as the backbone, and left the states the only remaining job: correcting the backbone’s residuals. Corrections were shrunk toward zero (k = 60, James-Stein flavor) across three conditionings, the nine GEX×RV cells, the VIX term-structure sign, and a state-arrival flag. The target moved from next-day to 5-day range, since version 1.5 had shown that daily noise drowns the signal, with leakage guards for the overlapping targets: training truncated at the refit boundary minus the horizon, HAC lags ≥ 10, and Duan smearing applied symmetrically to both models.

The specification was committed to version control before the run. Under a family deflator this is more than hygiene. Every sibling variant raises the family’s own bar, so the gate makes single-shot pre-registration the mathematically optimal research strategy. The incentive design does the disciplining.

The corrections did not improve the backbone (MAE 34.1 vs 33.7 bps; kill-test t = −1.07). The per-state decomposition shows the information is not uniformly zero: one high-gamma state gained 3.5 bps while a stressed state lost 4.5. A result found by inspecting the decomposition after the gate has failed is a hypothesis for a future mechanism, not a specification to refit, and it is logged as an open question.

5. Discussion: what stands after three versions

Dealer positioning carries real information relative to implied volatility alone, and that classifier remains in the vault. It does not add forecast value over a competent persistence, leverage, and implied-volatility ensemble at daily or weekly horizons under promotion criteria that require the edge to survive at half strength. A version 4 is warranted only if it brings a mechanism the ensemble structurally cannot carry. Event-conditioned transition rows (FOMC, CPI) are the standing candidate; such a model would face a bar near 3.1, because the deflator counts prior attempts against it.

Every result above is reproducible from the program records: the baseline ladder, the neural post-mortem, the gate-tightening memo, the pre-registration committed before results and verifiable by git timestamp, and the burial. The museum publishes the process because most research shops publish only the survivors.
